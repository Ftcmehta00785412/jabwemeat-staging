A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we meat.

Can I place an order yet?+

Not yet. This is a pre-launch storefront preview. You can explore products, inspect the configured packs and build a preview shopping bag, but no order is submitted and no payment is collected.

Where will JabWeMeat deliver?+

The pincode selector checks the areas configured in staging. A match is not a delivery promise: public ordering is still closed.

Are these the final products and prices?+

The displayed catalogue comes from the staging database and is not yet a final retail offer. Product photography, ingredients, allergens, sourcing information and prices must be approved before ordering opens.

How will delivery and payments work?+

Delivery slots, charges, payment methods and customer-support details will be published when the store launches. This preview does not accept card details, UPI payments or delivery addresses.
