A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

From choosing a cut to planning a delivery, here are the essentials about JabWeMeat. This is a pre-launch preview; the answers below distinguish current features from plans that are not yet confirmed.

Getting to know JabWeMeat

What is JabWeMeat?+

JabWeMeat is a meat-and-seafood storefront being built around everyday cooking and shared meals. Explore chicken, mutton, fish and seafood, eggs and ready-to-cook menu ideas in one place.

Is JabWeMeat accepting customer orders?+

Not yet. The website is a pre-launch preview. You can browse the catalogue and try the shopping bag, but customer ordering is closed. Any restricted payment testing is for authorised testers only and does not create a delivery order.

What should I check before choosing a product?+

Check the product name, pack weight, cut, bone-in or boneless option and availability. The current catalogue is provisional; final retail details must be confirmed before launch.

Are the website photographs pictures of the exact products?+

The current food images are illustrative serving suggestions, not verified photographs of the products you will receive. Approved product photography and accurate pack information are still being prepared.

Products, sourcing and food care

Where do JabWeMeat products come from?+

Supplier and sourcing information has not yet been finalised for publication. We will not describe products as farm-direct, sustainably sourced or locally sourced without supporting information.

Does JabWeMeat have published certifications or laboratory reports?+

No food-safety licence details, certifications or independent laboratory reports have been verified for publication on this preview. Do not interpret the website as making certified, organic, antibiotic-free or preservative-free claims.

Will products be chilled or frozen?+

This needs to be specified for each approved product. Do not assume that every item will be chilled or that every ready-to-cook item will be frozen. Check the final listing and pack label before buying.

How will I know the weight and cut of a pack?+

Use the product listing to check the configured weight and cut. Final listings should distinguish bone-in, boneless, whole and portioned options where applicable; the preview is not a confirmed retail offer.

Where can I find ingredients and allergen information?+

For prepared and marinated products, refer to the approved ingredient list, allergen declaration and pack label when available. Until those details are published, do not assume a product is suitable for an allergy or dietary restriction.

How should I store meat and seafood at home?+

Follow the product label first. As general guidance, refrigerate chilled perishables promptly at 4°C or below and keep frozen products at −18°C or below. Keep raw meat and seafood separate from ready-to-eat foods.

How long can I keep a product?+

Use the product-specific use-by date and storage instructions. Shelf life depends on the product, packaging and storage conditions; a single shelf-life promise cannot apply to the whole catalogue.

What is the safest way to thaw a frozen product?+

Follow its label. In general, thaw covered in the refrigerator, with a tray to contain drips, rather than leaving raw meat or seafood on the kitchen counter. Keep it separate from other food and cook as directed.

Are ready-to-cook products already cooked?+

Do not assume they are ready to eat. Marinated or prepared raw products still require thorough cooking. Follow the final product's cooking, storage and allergen instructions.

Shopping and delivery

How do I check whether my area is covered?+

Use the pincode selector to explore configured locations. During the preview, a pincode match is not a delivery commitment. Confirmed serviceable areas will be published before public ordering opens.

Can I submit an order from my shopping bag?+

You can explore products and adjust the preview bag, but it does not currently submit a customer delivery order. A payment test or a bag total should not be treated as an order confirmation.

Why is an item unavailable or missing?+

The preview reflects the configured catalogue and stock information, which may change during setup. We cannot promise a restock date or notification service at this stage.

What are the delivery charges and minimum order value?+

These have not yet been finalised. Applicable delivery charges, minimum-order rules and any taxes must be clearly shown before a customer commits to an order once ordering is enabled.

Can I choose a delivery time or set up repeat deliveries?+

Delivery schedules, slot availability and any repeat-order features are not confirmed yet. The preview does not reserve delivery slots or create recurring orders.

Can I change or cancel an order?+

There are no customer orders to change or cancel during the preview. The launch policy will explain the request process, cut-off times and any restrictions before purchases are enabled.

Payments and refunds

Which payment methods can customers use?+

Public payments are not enabled. Razorpay is configured for restricted test-mode checks only, and end-to-end verification remains incomplete. Live payment methods, including whether cash on delivery is offered, have not been confirmed.

Does a successful payment test mean my food is on the way?+

No. A test-mode result does not charge real money or create a delivery order. Customer order creation, stock handling and fulfilment must be verified separately before launch.

Should I enter real payment details for a test?+

No. Authorised testers should use only payment details approved for Razorpay test mode. Never share card details, bank passwords, OTPs or secret keys in chat or by email.

How will refunds and complaints be handled?+

Final cancellation, replacement and refund terms are still pending. No refund deadline or automatic replacement is promised in this preview. Once orders open, retain the order reference, product label and relevant photos when reporting an issue; do not consume a product you believe is unsafe.

Does JabWeMeat offer cashback or a paid membership?+

No cashback wallet, loyalty benefit or paid membership is currently offered through this preview. Any future offer will need its own published terms.

Accounts and getting help

Why might sign-in not work yet?+

Google sign-in setup is still pending, and email sign-in may be subject to sending limits. Avoid repeated code requests if a limit appears. Customer sign-in does not grant access to the admin dashboard.

How can I contact JabWeMeat?+

The intended support address is support@jabwemeat.com, but forwarding setup is not complete, so please do not rely on it yet. Confirmed support channels and business contact information will be published before launch.
